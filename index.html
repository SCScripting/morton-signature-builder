Install it

        

          
          
          
        


        

          
    
            
  1. Click Copy signature above.
    2. 
            
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
    3. 
            
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature → Create new. Name it (for example, "Morton").
    4. 
            
  4. Click inside the signature box and paste with Ctrl+V (Windows) or ⌘+V (Mac).
    5. 
            
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for For new emails use and On reply/forward use. Tip: build a second one with the Reply (compact) layout and use that for replies.
    6. 
            
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Send yourself a test email.
    7. 
          

          
Image not showing? Gmail blocks images that aren't at a public link. Check the logo or headshot URL opens in a private browser window. Gmail also caps signatures at 10,000 characters; the counter under the preview keeps track.

        


        

          
    
            
  1. The Gmail app on iPhone and Android only supports a plain-text signature. It does not use the one you set on your computer.
    2. 
            
  2. Open the Gmail app → menu (☰) → Settings → pick your account.
    3. 
            
  3. iPhone: Signature settings → turn on Mobile Signature. Android: tap Mobile signature.
    4. 
            
  4. Type a short version, for example your name, title, office, and phone number.
    5. 
          

          
Emails sent from the Gmail website on your phone's browser, or from a computer, will still use the full signature.
